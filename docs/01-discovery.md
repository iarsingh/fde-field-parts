# Discovery — Helios Equipment

The user is the depot lead on the night shift. A tech calls with a fault code. Today the lead opens three spreadsheets: the asset list, the fault-to-part sheet they signed, and the bin count.

Constraint: the depot network is not allowed to call a parts vendor. Creating a purchase order is a buyer workflow, not a lookup.

Out of scope: ordering, warranty claims, and recommending a part that is not on the signed fault list for that model.
